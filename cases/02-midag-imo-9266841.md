# Case 02 – MIDAG (ex-MIDAS) / IMO 9266841

**Research focus:** False flag usage, recent name change, loading history at Russian terminal, US sanctions linkage, EU IRINI boarding confirmation.

## Key Event (November 2026)

On **13 November 2026**, personnel from the EU naval operation **EUNAVFOR MED IRINI** boarded the tanker **MV MIDAG** in the Mediterranean Sea under Article 110 of UNCLOS.

The inspection confirmed that the vessel was sailing under a **false Zimbabwe flag**.

EU High Representative Kaja Kallas publicly announced the operation on 15 November 2026 and linked the vessel to Russia’s shadow fleet. The vessel was allowed to continue its voyage after inspection.

## Vessel Particulars

- **IMO:** 9266841
- **Type:** Crude / Oil Products Tanker
- **Length overall:** approx. 241 m
- **Deadweight:** approx. 106,138 tonnes
- **Current reported flag (false at time of boarding):** Zimbabwe
- **Registered owner / commercial manager (as of late 2026 reporting):** Xuanyue Shipping & Trading Co. (Liberia)

## Identity & Flag History (publicly reported)

- Previously named **MIDAS**
- Previously sailed under **Panama** flag
- Renamed **MIDAG** around July 2026
- From approximately July 2026, commercial databases listed the Zimbabwe flag as false

## Loading History of Interest

In **May 2026**, while still named MIDAS and under Panama flag, the vessel called at Russian Far East ports:
- Arrived near Vostochny on 5 May 2026
- Docked at **Kozmino oil terminal** the following day and remained for nearly 23 hours

## Sanctions Context

The vessel was placed under **US sanctions** in connection with the transportation of Iranian oil. The previous owner entity was also designated.

## Analytical Notes

This case illustrates several recurring patterns:

1. **Name change** after high-risk activity or designation.
2. **False flag** usage to complicate jurisdiction and interdiction.
3. **Ownership opacity** through rapid corporate changes.
4. **Mixed cargo history** (Russian terminal loading + Iranian oil sanctions linkage).
5. **Operational continuity** despite boarding and public exposure.

## Status

Open case note – further ownership chain, beneficial ownership and post-boarding AIS behaviour remain under observation.
