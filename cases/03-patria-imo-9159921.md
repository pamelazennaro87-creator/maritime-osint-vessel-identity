# Case 03 – PATRIA / IMO 9159921

**Research focus:** Sanctioned Ro-Ro vessel with prior weapons-transport reporting, repeated West African port shuttle (Douala–Owendo), prolonged anchorage behaviour, ownership linked to Sakhalin entities.

## Key Identifiers

- **IMO:** 9159921
- **MMSI:** 273394340
- **Call sign:** UBVP6
- **Type:** Ro-Ro Cargo Ship / Multipurpose
- **Built:** 1999 (Pendik Shipyard, Turkey)
- **Length overall:** approx. 101 m
- **Gross tonnage:** 5,825
- **Deadweight:** approx. 5,795 t
- **Current flag:** Russian Federation

## Sanctions Status

- United States (OFAC) – July 2023
- Ukraine (GUR War & Sanctions)
- Canada – June 2026

Ukraine lists the vessel under the category of transportation of military cargo / weapons and military equipment.

## Ownership & Management (as reported in public sanctions and vessel databases)

- **Registered owner / ISM manager:** Hanro Shipping – Sakhalin Projects LLC (Russia)
- **Commercial manager:** PJSC Sakhalin Shipping Company (SASCO)

Previous flag history includes Antigua & Barbuda and Germany before the shift to the Russian flag.

## Documented Activity (public OSINT – Bellingcat & AIS/satellite cross-checks)

### 2026 West Africa pattern
From March 2026 onward the vessel has conducted repeated shuttles between:

- **Port of Owendo / Libreville, Gabon** (apparent loading – draught increases)
- **Port of Douala, Cameroon** (apparent unloading – draught decreases)

Bellingcat (August 2026) documented at least four complete rotations using AIS + Planet satellite imagery. The vessel also spent extended periods at anchorage off Lagos, Nigeria, and shorter periods off Equatorial Guinea.

Draught analysis by Bellingcat showed consistent pattern: deeper after Owendo calls, shallower after Douala calls.

### Prior reporting
Radio France International previously reported that *Patria* was one of two vessels that delivered weapons to Conakry (Guinea) intended for the Kremlin-controlled Africa Corps operating in Mali.

### Later positions (public trackers)
- October 2026: returned to Douala area (Planet imagery and AIS confirmations published by Bellingcat and secondary reporting).
- Early November 2026: still associated with Douala / Douala Anchorage area according to public AIS aggregators.

## Analytical Notes

1. **Not a classic oil shadow-fleet tanker** – this is a Ro-Ro / multipurpose cargo vessel. The pattern is logistical (possible dual-use or military-related cargo) rather than oil price-cap evasion.
2. **Unusual commercial behaviour** – prolonged multi-month presence in a small West African port cluster without a clear point-to-point commercial cycle has been flagged by independent analysts as atypical.
3. **Continuity of Russian state-linked logistics** – ownership and management remain within the Sakhalin Shipping / Hanro structure after sanctions.
4. **Open questions** – exact nature of cargo moved between Gabon and Cameroon, reason for multi-week Lagos anchorage, and current operational status remain unverified in open sources.
