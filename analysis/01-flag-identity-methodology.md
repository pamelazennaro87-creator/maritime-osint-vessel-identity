# Flag & Identity Methodology

(Adapted from portfolio research discipline on vessel identity reconstruction)

## Research Question Template

How can the identity and historical trajectory of a vessel be reconstructed when its name, flag, management and associated corporate entities change over time?

## Analytical Approach

1. **Anchor on IMO**  
   The IMO number is the primary persistent identifier. All name and flag changes are mapped against it.

2. **Document the sequence**  
   Record every documented name and flag in chronological order. Do not collapse or “clean” the history.

3. **Treat discrepancies as data**  
   Different databases frequently show conflicting owner, manager or flag information for the same period. These discrepancies are preserved and examined rather than resolved by preference.

4. **Separate layers**  
   - Verified (primary/official source)  
   - Corroborated (independent sources agree)  
   - Unverified (single source or unconfirmed)  
   - Analytical inference (reasoned conclusion that must not be presented as fact)

5. **Ownership & management mapping**  
   Track both registered owner and commercial/technical manager. Rapid changes in either are treated as signals.

6. **Sanctions context**  
   Record the date and authority of any designation (US, EU, UK, Ukraine, etc.) and note whether the vessel continued operating after designation.

## Key Questions Applied to Every Case

- Who is the same entity across different records?
- Which information refers to different periods?
- Which relationships are directly documented?
- Which conclusions require additional corroboration?
- What would disprove the current working hypothesis?

## Core Principle

The objective is not the most dramatic answer. The objective is the most defensible answer supported by the available evidence.
